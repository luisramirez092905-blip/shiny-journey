Four-leg Jansen walking mechanism

A four-legged walker built on Theo Jansen's eight-bar leg linkage, all driven from one crank. I pulled the motion data to check the gait with numbers instead of eyeballing the animation.

  • About 20 leg links, one degree of freedom, 60 RPM input
  • Paired legs run exactly out of phase for a stable gait
  • Out-of-plane motion stays at zero, so the mates are right

CPU heatsink thermal analysis

A steady-state thermal study of a finned aluminum heatsink under a 50 W CPU load, to see where the heat goes and where a fan would help most.

  • Peaks at 63.9 °C, below the 85–100 °C throttling range
  • Only 16.4 K difference across the whole part
  • Center fin channels run hottest, the best place to aim a fan

Gyroscopic cup holder

A self-leveling cup holder for bikes and wheelchairs. Two gimbal rings let gravity keep the drink upright as the mount tilts. Team project with Ghazi Mustafa. I modeled both rings.

  • Printed and assembled in PLA for about $10
  • Printed fits came out too tight, so we revised the pin holes from 0.175 to 0.2 in
  • Design targets: cups 64–100 mm, under 1 kg total
